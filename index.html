Emergency close and controlled recovery

Recovery is not one click. It needs admin verification first, then an authorised recovery, and the recovery is recorded.

Protected shutdown: two modes

Pick a mode first. Autonomous: two customer officers approve. Joint management: the customer owner and the vendor approve. All approvals are needed before a controlled shutdown. During shutdown the vault stays locked: gate off does not open the vault. Mode selection here is a presenter tool only: a real deployment mode change needs approval through the existing authorisation mechanism, and an ordinary admin cannot switch the authorisation regime alone. After a shutdown, a device restart alone never resumes the service: resumption needs its own authorisation.
